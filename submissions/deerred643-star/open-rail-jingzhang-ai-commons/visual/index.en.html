Red-Team Review: Objections & Failure-Mode Early Warning (new in v2.0, concept)

  • Voluntary critical audit: 5 strongest objections (gentrification / AI gimmickry / key-area vacancy / one-off events / governance as formality) answered one by one, citing only mechanisms already in the package

  • Division of labour: risk.json = compliance & material risks; failure-modes.json = outcome-level failure risks, cross-referenced; all indicators stay "pending baseline", failed samples never dropped (machine-readable: visual/assets/failure-modes.json)

Self-Referential Proof (II): AI-Assisted Urban-Design Workflow (new in v2.1, concept)

  • The package argues "AI participates in civic co-creation"; its own production is the minimal viable proof: taskbook/announcement → open scaffold → geometry generation (Python/shapely/pyproj, EPSG:4548) → figure rendering (Pillow) → four-gate self-check (self_check.json) → human review — every stage openly inspectable
  • Responsibility division: the AI handles structure and reproducibility; the human (Yu Feng, deerred643-star) is accountable for facts, citations, copyright and final expression; provisional boundaries, unknown regulatory indicators and downgraded sources are each human-reviewed
  • Discipline: disclosing the pipeline does not claim AI design has reached professional depth; all facts cross-verifiable against agent.json / self_check.json / manifest.json (machine-readable: visual/assets/ai-workflow-pipeline.json)

Public participation & equity (concept framework)

  • Participation ladder: disclosure → consultation (Issues/PRs + workshops) → co-design (resident workshops) → co-decision (public seats at the 90-day review)
  • Three-tier negotiation: on-site hall mediation (same day) → community council (7 working days) → professional arbitration (15 working days); conclusions written back to cards
  • Equity baseline indicators: barrier-free coverage / equivalent-path accessibility / median complaint response — measured per node via the SEB schema during pilots, failed samples never dropped

Assumptions

  • A-CONTROLS-001 (pending_professional_confirmation): public reporting indicates the HD00-1601 block-level regulatory plan was approved on 2026-08-11 (not yet in approved_formal_sources; treated as background pending registry review, not a statutory basis); the plan's own indicator values, road redlines, property rights, municipal pipelines and engineering conditions must still be confirmed by professional bodies once official attachments are obtained.
    All spatial content in this package is a concept proposal/reference scheme; FAR, building height and retain-renovate-demolish statutory controls remain unknown, forming no approved conclusions.
  • A-BOUNDARY-002 (provisional_in_use): the overall design boundary and the three key areas use the repository's provisional_boundaries.geojson (inferred from the announcement's textual extents and area constraints, provisional_rough).
    All geometry layers and metrics such as site_area_sqm, green_ratio and public_space_ratio are interim design-model values; once official precise boundaries are released, all layers and metrics must be recalculated along the registered path.
  • A-DERIVED-003 (design_assumption): the Open Rail corridor, hall squares, land-use zoning, building footprints and road centrelines are concept design layers generated by this agent (design_proposal); alignments and shapes are schematic.
    Layers support review of design intent and metric magnitude, and must not be cited as road redlines, parcel boundaries or engineering alignments.
  • A-CRS-004 (method_note): the GeoJSON exchange CRS is EPSG:4326; area and length recalculation uses EPSG:4548 (CGCS2000 three-degree zone, central meridian 117E); projecting from 4326 to 4548 introduces millimetre-level differences.
    Recalculated results may differ from registered values by less than 0.01% — normal projection and coordinate-rounding (7 decimal places) error.
  • A-COMPOSITE-005 (design_assumption): the public-space and green-space layers intentionally overlap (riverside green belts double as public open space; pocket plazas sit inside community parks); the two layers' area metrics are computed independently and must not be summed.
    green_ratio and public_space_ratio correspond to their own layers; summing them would overstate total open space; a merged calibration must be redefined at the professional deepening stage.
