N7-1f · 3 loops

Complete visitor-service loop

Active opt-in → pre-arrival task pack → Dazhongsi arrival and staffed foyer → luggage, connectivity, payment, lodging, transport and food → event and city overview → Jing-Zhang cultural route → Zhongzhiyuan public observation edge / AI Origin public-experience edge → return to the place of stay → continue or depart → close temporary tasks and permissions.

Ordinary route
Without digital service, public transport, physical signs, paper maps, telephones, staffed help and accessible routes remain.
Stop line
The task pack does not become a visitor profile; visitors are not test sensors and do not yield to devices; real residents, households and homes are not exhibits.
Sites, opening information, operators, routes and receiving institutions remain subject to field and institutional verification.

Local merchant, youth and older-adult economy loop

Real need → merchant open service card → public verification of bounded facts → private Agent match for this order → direct user–merchant trade → routine service near AI Origin → Zhongzhiyuan diagnosis, retest and training for complex faults → capability returns to the community and the next order.

Public boundary
Publish facts, not paid organic rank; keep ads separate, take no share for the basic match, do not capture the customer relationship, and retain correction and appeal.
Livelihood
Younger and older workers may take paid installation, repair, reception, assistance, mentoring, reinspection or guiding roles within qualification limits.
Platforms, merchant registers, orders, jobs, revenue, insurance and operating relationships remain proposals to test.

Smart classroom—pickup—after-school—household chain

Teacher preparation support → teacher-centred classroom → lightweight same-day learning pack → guardian selects a lawful, staffed and available named pickup seat → single-use authorization → school-side human check and the child's right to refuse → human physical handoff → walking group / compliant vehicle → named after-school receipt → food, rest, play and learning as needed → guardian / next human receipt → close the day's task and permissions.

Responsibility
An app task is not release. If the named person is absent or the child refuses, responsibility stays with the school-side human.
Privacy
No persistent face watching or personality-like child score; household disagreement, emotion and private life do not flow back into school by default.
Timetables, eligibility, background checks, insurance, pricing, pickup duties and operators remain unverified.

Co-payment, three-area and Jing-Zhang boundaries

The “about 30% public + about 70% household” split is only a non-binding policy prototype for pickup-service co-payment—not a budget, price, subsidy commitment, implementation ratio or new metric. Any later use requires separate costing, tiering, disclosure and decision. The loops feed one another across Zhongzhiyuan, the AI Origin Community and Dazhongsi, while each area keeps its own responsibility, degraded mode and exit. Jing-Zhang is a non-central public interface strip, not a fourth key area, central brain or private-data spine. JZ-03 is the exceptional screenless human-relay branch for an outage. Maintenance crosses all three loops but does not become a fourth headline loop.

P01

P01 | Verify the public line, blue-green continuity, test edge and recovery route first. Only then may public-space/landscape, test-safety and maintenance roles open a small reversible pilot; any uncontrolled crossing or ecological/access conflict stops it.

P02

P02 | First drill hard stop, de-energisation, isolation and mechanical recovery for one bounded test. Open only with verified device, window, weather/surface, responsibility and permission; powered return is prohibited after a hard stop.

Phase 3 | hard stop, isolation, recovery and maintenance evidence precede any expansion of testing

P03

P03 | Verify equipment, work orders, isolation, MEP/fire, read-only diagnosis, human retest and appeal closure before locating the backstage. Mismatch, excess authority, failed retest or absent receiver keeps the item isolated.

Phase 2-3 | repair, isolation, energy and retest capacity precede scaled device operation

P04

P04 | Verify the real gate, timetable and three-segment human responsibility, then walk, observe four 15-minute windows and run a no-AI handoff drill. Refusal, unclear identity, unsafe route or absent receiver returns the child to staffed school-side waiting.

Phase 1-2 | ordinary passage and named human handoff first; reversible safety micro-renewal before optional intelligent assistance

P05

P05 | First inventory real visible help gates, ordinary phones, staffed windows and accessibility/fire/maintenance conditions. Community help is not medical receipt; professional responsibility moves only after explicit acceptance by a qualified human.

Phase 1-2 | reuse existing public-service ground floors and staffed entrances before adding optional digital assistance

P06

P06 | Verify shared-side lighting, backup power, mechanical bypass, phones, paper contacts and human duty, then run a no-AI outage drill. Publish no residence location, and restored power does not reactivate closed permission.

Phase 1-2 | protect ordinary/emergency lighting, mechanical bypass and staffed duty before reversible resilience interfaces

P07

P07 | Verify real exits, four-quadrant destinations, ordinary/accessible routes, crossings, egress and responsibility segments, then walk every route. Any access, accessibility, egress or maintenance obstruction stops the work.

Phase 1-2 | preserve ordinary station-city movement, accessibility and egress before ground stitching

P08

P08 | Establish credential-free ordinary passage and staffed/quiet assistance before testing an optional digital line. Station, road, property, desk and receiving institution own separate segments; ordinary passage continues when digital service is refused, fails or is withdrawn.

Phase 1-2 | establish a credential-free staffed foyer before optional bounded digital access

P09

P09 | Verify the resident quiet route, ordinary passage, loading, egress, maintenance and complete exit before a small reversible event. Any blocked route, missing owner, expired window or incomplete exit closes and clears it.

Phase 3-4 | reversible events only after resident quiet-route, egress, noise, operation and complete-exit evidence closes

P10

P10 | Verify each physical interface, ordinary phone/paper/staffed window, roster, referral, complaint, audit, incident and closure locally. Accept only the bounded task—not a whole person or household—and never become central command.

Cross-phase | nodes form with their local spatial projects while always retaining domain separation, human service and offline capability
