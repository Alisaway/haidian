# XIAOYUE RIVER SCENARIO WING: AI Scenario Roadshow Corridor & Public Experience Trail (Concept)

**English convenience summary** (full binding narrative is the Chinese proposal.md; this summary may lag the zh text).

以小月河滨水岸线为轴，组织约3公里连续AI场景路演长廊与公共体验路径：一处滨水路演广场、三处公共体验节点（AI科普驿站、场景试验台、社区共验场）沿水岸布置，以完整步行体验路径串联，把AI场景展示嵌入河道日常公共生活。滨水以生态修复与高可达公共空间为主，设施模块化可逆、含夜间路演与离线人工替代路径。全部为概念建议、参考方案，基于 provisional 边界，官方数据发布后复算。

## Outline
- Three-Layer Scope: coordinated research (AI-coupled industry & future-city; "scenario-first, ecology-second, industry-third" sequencing) / overall design (~3 km riverside corridor with one-block-deep hinterland, concept) / key-area detailed design (1 roadshow square + 3 experience nodes + corridor segments).
- Roadshow Square: riverside stage + meeting lounge + prefab terraces + waterfront rest platform.
- Node 2 - AI Science Station: interactive exhibition & education along campus and school routes.
- Node 3 - Community Co-Lab Ground: elderly/child friendly AI services with offline human fallback.
- 12 scenario slots in 3 tiers: permanent (AI water-quality visualization, smart path lighting, accessible audio guide), quarterly rotation (education, health, culture, virtual tours), pop-up for roadshow events; offline human fallback at every station.
- 5 categories of renewal projects in 3 phases (1-2 / 3-5 / 5-8 years, concept).
- All figures are concept suggestions on a provisional boundary; recompute when official data is published.
