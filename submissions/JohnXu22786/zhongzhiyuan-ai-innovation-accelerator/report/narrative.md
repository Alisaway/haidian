# Formal Narrative

This package retains the taskbook's three positioning statements, five functions, three areas/two wings and the provisional-data gap. The spatial concept is organized as one belt, two chains and a cross-three-area interface network: L1 Chain Harbor Full-Stack Flagship Park maps to the Zhongzhiyuan AI Autonomous Innovation Acceleration Area, L2 Star-Track Co-creation Community maps to the Beijing AI Origin Community, and L3 Compute-Pivot Chain Station maps to the Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster. These are three conceptual mappings, not three confirmed sites inside Zhongzhiyuan.

The east-west stitching, north-south continuity and Dazhongsi smart-native scene are now mapped as concept-only spatial types with explicit study-area/area conventions, operators, public-access boundaries, approval prerequisites and stop conditions. No new precise area or engineering conclusion is claimed before official boundary, heritage, transport, ownership and safety data are available.

Reader-facing area text is reduced to about 11.41 million sqm / 1141 ten-thousand sqm. The provisional boundary is not an official redline; official boundary, existing-land survey or statutory control data triggers a full recalculation.

The AI ecosystem contains S1-S12 scenario cards. T1 is model public evaluation, T2 is the barrier-free accompaniment test, and T3 is long-term drift monitoring. The five global cases are compared at project level in the proposal and `sources.json`; only project-page facts are used, and no partnership, endorsement, image/logo reuse or rights clearance is implied.
