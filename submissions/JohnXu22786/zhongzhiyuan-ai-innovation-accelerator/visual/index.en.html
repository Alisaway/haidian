AI scenarios and task coverage

Twelve scenario cards: smart R&D collaboration and shared labs, model training and compute dispatch, public evaluation and benchmarks, data-element sandbox, AI expo and roadshow center, logistics sorting and drone delivery, barrier-free accompaniment, crowd-flow sensing dispatch, campus shuttle and autonomous buses, AI training and talent station, cultural guide and century timeline, community deliberation and resident services. All data anonymized and aggregated with human review; no individual identification or tracking.

Three industry test protocols (model evaluation, data quality, runtime monitoring) and five persona groups are in the AI chapter of the proposal; AI technical protocols run model benchmarks, data-quality checks, error stratification, false-positive monitoring, runtime monitoring and human takeover.

The compliance matrix covers 23 tasks (announcement 1.3-1.5 and agent open call 1-6); standard and design-depth matrices are in the corresponding JSON files.

Sources and risks

Data sources follow sources.json (verifiable entries + self-generated provisional geometry + per-case global case sources); risks are declared honestly in the risk chapter and risk.json: geometry, ownership and approvals, low-altitude and shuttle, data governance, brand prior rights. Three-level scope

Coordinated research area: 43.6 km2 (official text figure, industry and future-city research); overall design area: 11.4 km2 (official text figure, urban renewal and regulatory-plan-level urban design at concept depth); key areas: 368.4 ha total (official text figure), with this package deepening the Zhongzhiyuan AI Autonomous Innovation Accelerator (~192 ha); the Beijing AI Origin Community and the Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster are studied only as interface zones.

Land use

Concept land-use structure (provisional, recomputed when official scope is published): R&D land first, business and finance second, with youth housing, commercial, cultural, education, transport, park-green and reserved land as concept ranges; shares are directional aggregate ranges only, with the aggregation rule and recompute triggers in the metrics chapter of the proposal.

Mobility

Rail plus campus shuttle: connection around Qinghe, Zhichunlu and Dazhongsi stations (per public line information) with autonomous buses and on-demand minibuses as concept carriers; slow traffic first; low-altitude and shuttle approvals are unsettled, no conclusions preset. Municipal works reserve green-compute supply and heat-recovery interfaces.

Blue-green network and public space

The heritage-park green spine plus the Xiaoyuehe corridor integrates blue-green space; platform memories, sleeper paving and a century timeline shape the cultural thread; public-space facilities run as a reversible modular component library (assemble - use - withdraw), and the character guideline blends industrial-heritage vocabulary with light AI-era interfaces.

Buildings

Retain-led renewal: heritage park fabric and historic remains are kept first; existing buildings convert to R&D/incubation and shared labs; only individually justified buildings are demolished; new construction is concentrated in three accents - flagship gateway, pilot facilities and the expo-roadshow building - strictly controlled in scale; building scale and FAR are not preset (concept scope).

Renewal projects

Three-phase renewal list: near (1-3y) opening season, retain-led replacement, flagship gateway and Star-Track community pilots; mid (3-5y) three nodes take shape, shared lab network and shuttle loop; far (5-10y) full-stack ecosystem and evaluation-dialogue platform mature. The implementation and long-term operation matrix lists phase, actors, dependent data, approval and safety prerequisites, pilot scope, cost tier, KPI, human takeover, stop conditions, public feedback and conversion paths per project.

Self-check status

Self-check results follow self_check.json: after passing the valroot gates (spatial/visual/professional/self-check), this package records each result and known minor notices (provisional boundary class).

Assumptions

Key assumptions are registered in assumptions.json: provisional boundary, unpublished rail protection and control conditions, unknown compute and power supply conditions, sensing data processed as anonymized aggregates only; each is re-verified and recomputed when official data arrives.
