Key Areas

Yixinglang (Zhongzhiyuan side), Yiyangxiaoyuan (AI Origin Community side), Zhilingguan (Dazhongsi side) implementation cards: site selection, verification, components, human fallback, licenses to verify, operation, pilot KPIs, stop/adjust conditions.

Slow Mobility

The Yixinglang axis links the three nodes with barrier-free connections to rail and bus ends; rest nodes at concept intervals; rail alignments not judged.

Blue-Green Public Space

Green-belt axis with node parks; low-intervention, reversible facilities; honor-display and science interfaces open to the public with human fallback.

Buildings

Keep-first renovation and functional replacement with lightweight reversible aging-friendly interfaces; demolition only for case-by-case scattered buildings; no large new buildings.

Renewal Projects

Four project categories; RACI lead-collaborate; near term 1-3 yrs pilot block, mid 3-5 yrs three-node network, far 5-10 yrs along-the-belt replication; pilot stop/exit conditions and annual review activities (Silver Classroom Season, Home Safety Week, Intergenerational Day, Smart Elder-Care Festival, Developer Co-Creation Camp, Yixingloop Run Day).
