# GLOBAL AI ECOSYSTEM ATLAS - CASE MAPPING AND INTERACTIVE PLATFORM (Concept)

**English convenience summary** (full binding narrative is the Chinese proposal.md; this summary may lag the zh text).

以「全球AI创新生态案例图谱（ATLAS·JZ）」沿京张铁路原线绿带与遗址公园组织全球AI创新生态案例图谱化的公共展示与互动平台概念：把全球创新城市与园区的生态案例转译为可查阅、可比较、可共创的图谱空间，「一带三馆」——图谱馆（案例数据可视化馆）、生态雷达站（全球创新生态动态监测装置）、共创图谱廊（公众可编辑的图谱共创墙），五类AI+场景（案例数据AI清洗与语义检索、图谱可视化自动生成、多语案例讲解与翻译、数据更新巡检AI、案例引用与内容审核人工复核）仅处理匿名聚合数据、关键决策人工复核、禁止过度监控，交通慢行优先、绿带连续慢行轴贯通节点，分期实施+年度活动体系（图谱更新发布日、全球案例研习周、图谱共创工作坊）。全部为概念建议、参考方案，基于 provisional 边界，官方数据发布后复算。

## Outline
- Global AI Ecosystem Atlas (ATLAS.JZ): using the Jingzhang heritage green belt as the backbone to turn global innovation-ecosystem experience into a searchable, comparable, co-creatable map space.
- Node 1 - Atlas Pavilion: case-data visualisation pavilion presenting global AI-ecosystem cases in charts, physical exhibits and digital interfaces, filterable by city, park and theme.
- Node 2 - Ecosystem Radar Station: public-art style dynamic monitoring installation showing anonymized aggregated ecosystem vitality trends.
- Node 3 - Co-creation Gallery: publicly editable atlas co-creation wall linking physical and online contributions, with human-reviewed content moderation.
- 5 AI+ scenarios (case-data AI cleaning and semantic search, automatic atlas visualisation, multilingual case interpretation and translation, data refresh inspection AI, human-reviewed case citation and content review); anonymized aggregation only, key decisions human-reviewed, no over-surveillance.
- Slow-traffic-first: a continuous slow-traffic axis along the green belt links the three nodes with surrounding campuses, parks and rail stations; annual events: atlas update day, global case study week, atlas co-creation workshop.
- All figures are concept suggestions on a provisional boundary; recompute when official data is published.
