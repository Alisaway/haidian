Overview Map

ATLAS·JZ organizes a public atlas platform of global AI innovation ecosystem cases along the Jing-Zhang railway heritage green belt. All geometry is based on the organizer provisional boundary (not a redline). Official text calibers: coordinated research about 43.6 km2 / overall design about 11.4 km2 / key areas about 368.4 ha; this package sits inside the overall design area with the three key areas as nodes.

Three-Level Scope

The three levels transfer progressively and share the indicator and compliance matrices. The three-zones-two-wings structure (AI Origin Community, Zhongzhiyuan AI Acceleration Area, Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster; Zhongguancun Tech-Service Wing, Xiaoyuehe Scenario Wing) and the regional synergy loop (Beiwai Community, Future Science City, Huairou Science City, E-Town, Beijing-Tianjin-Hebei) are concept mechanisms; recompute after official geometry release.

Key Areas

One belt, three pavilions: Atlas Pavilion (Zhongzhiyuan AI Acceleration Area; case-data visualization), Ecosystem Radar Station (AI Origin Community; anonymized vitality monitoring) and Co-creation Gallery (Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster; publicly editable atlas wall). The three nodes are also three atlas landmarks, with authorized Honor Column credits.

Land Use

Conceptual land-use scenario ranges per the land_use layer (range values, not current statistics): green/open 18-25%, education/research 26-31%, industry/office 13-18%, commercial 8-12%, residential 10-14%, others 6-10%. Retain-renovate-demolish follows retain first, renovate second, demolish case by case; recompute after official land data.

Transport & Slow Traffic

The continuous green-belt slow axis links the three nodes with rail stations; walking and cycling first. East-west stitching streets plus the north-south spine form the conceptual "stitch and connect" strategy; event crowds are time-zoned; AI transport scenarios are anonymized, human-reviewed and free of over-surveillance.

Blue-Green & Public Space

The heritage green belt is the backbone connecting small green spaces and open space. The Atlas Public Space Component Library supports converting exhibition, installation and writing nodes into markets, lectures and daily leisure; temporary facilities are modular, demountable and recyclable; signage stays restrained.

Buildings

Conceptual building units focus on functional renewal of stock (exhibition, office, display and support). No building-scale conclusions are given; demolition is limited to case-by-case scattered structures; no FAR or height conclusions are provided.

Renewal Projects

Phasing: near-term (1-3 years) Atlas Pavilion pilot plus digital atlas platform with stage gates; mid-term (3-5 years) Radar Station, Co-creation Gallery and slow-axis connection; long-term (5-10 years) whole-belt atlas network and annual operations. Three annual event brands: Atlas Update Release Day, Global Case Study Week and Atlas Co-creation Workshop; every stage has stop conditions and exit mechanisms.

AI Scenarios

AI innovation ecosystem atlas: three zones-two wings x factor mechanisms x 8 sourced benchmark cases; 10 scenario cards plus 3 industry test-verification scenarios. AI governance trio: anonymized aggregation only, human review of key decisions, no over-surveillance. Technical protocols: model evaluation, data quality, error stratification, runtime monitoring.

Assumptions

Geometry is a provisional boundary (A-BOUNDARY-001); statutory controls (green/blue lines, heritage, municipal capacity) are not assumed before publication (A-CONTROLS-001); case data is cited as anonymized public information (A-DATA-001); no individual-identifying collection in AI scenarios (A-PRIVACY-001); events and phasing are suggestions, not arranged commitments (A-EVENT-001).
