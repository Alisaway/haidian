Three-tier scope

Coordinated research scope: industry and future-city direction, indicator-governance environment and regional coordination judgments (official text caliber 43.6 km²). Overall design scope: approx. 11.4 km² (this package's conceptual subset, provisional; green-belt spine and three-node conceptual layout). Key areas: detailed design of the Indicator Hub, Monitoring Post and Disclosure Pavilion (approx. 368.4 ha caliber). All boundaries are provisional; recompute after official data release.

Key areas

Three nodes (conceptual sites): Indicator Hub (mid-belt; aggregation, recomputation, publication), Monitoring Post (belt segments; collection, inspection, maintenance), Disclosure Pavilion (near neighborhoods and rail stations; disclosure, interpretation, anonymous feedback). A three-tier “landmark catalog” (hub/corridor/node) with an honor-display area; the node-to-three-areas/two-wings loop is verified through annual recomputation. Sites must be relocated after professional review.

Land use

Conceptual land-use structure (provisional, single caliber: category area ÷ this package's provisional boundary area; recomputed after official release): green and open space 33%; research and industry 25%; public administration and services 14%; commercial and business 12%; residential 11%; transport and station 3%; reserved and flexible 2%.

Slow traffic

Slow-traffic priority: a continuous slow-traffic spine links the Indicator Hub, Monitoring Posts and Disclosure Pavilion; street sections respond in time-shared mode to daily and festival states; connections to corridor universities, parks and rail stations are improved (walking and cycling first); rail stations and monitoring nodes connect smoothly, prioritizing walking links to the green belt.

Blue-green space

“Green belt as axis, node parks as pearls”: the heritage-park green belt forms the blue-green spine linking small green spaces and open spaces along the corridor; monitoring and disclosure facilities are low-intervention, reversible and relocatable (component library), without blocking heritage views; the character continues the Jing-Zhang railway industrial-memory vocabulary in dialogue with an ordered data atmosphere.

Buildings

Retain/renovate/demolish: retention first, preserving the heritage-park texture and railway relics; existing buildings undergo functional conversion with reserved indicator-monitoring conversion directions (collection interfaces and disclosure screen positions); demolition limited to scattered small buildings verified case-by-case; nodes are modular, reversible and light-touch, no large new volumes (conceptual caliber, no building scale presupposed).

Renewal projects

Pilot plan (concept): near term (1–3 yr) baseline plus pilot Hub and Pavilion; mid term (3–5 yr) Post network and AI monitoring/alerting; long term (5–10 yr) routine supervision and annual recomputation; includes KPIs, decision gates, exit/rollback and annual recomputation flows; annual programs: Indicator Open Day, Implementation Progress Briefing, Monitoring Post Experience Week.

AI scenarios

Five AI+ scenarios (detailed in 10 scenario cards): collection-and-aggregation AI, progress-monitoring AI, deviation-alerting AI (backbone, each with human review), disclosure-interpretation AI, public-feedback AI (supporting); anonymized aggregates only, free text desensitized before processing, no individual-identification tracking, no excessive monitoring; human review of key decisions; three industry validation test scenarios (caliber consistency, alert accuracy, disclosure readability).
