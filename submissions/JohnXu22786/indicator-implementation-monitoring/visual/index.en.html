Transport & Slow Mobility

Slow-traffic-first: a continuous spine links the three nodes; streets time-share daily/festival states; rail stations and monitoring nodes connect smoothly; facilities are placed along slow-traffic space with low intervention. All conceptual mechanism suggestions; no engineering values or implementation conclusions.

Blue-Green Public Space

"Green belt as axis, node parks as pearls": the heritage-park belt links small green spaces and open spaces; monitoring/disclosure facilities blend into parks - light, transparent, reversible - without blocking heritage views or belt sightlines; restrained signage; all-age inclusiveness (barrier-free access, large-print disclosure, tactile/voice guidance, child-friendly and age-friendly spaces), with inclusive arrangements reserved for vulnerable groups (scope per the Barrier-Free Environment Law).

Buildings

Retention first: preserve the heritage-park texture and railway relics; existing buildings undergo functional conversion with reserved indicator-monitoring conversion directions (collection interfaces, disclosure screen positions); monitoring and public-service facilities are inserted lightly, reversibly and modularly, preferably combined with station houses, management rooms, station auxiliary spaces and under-bridge spaces, avoiding new land occupation and large-scale rebuilding. No floor-area, building-height or retain/renovate/demolish conclusions are presupposed.

Renewal Projects

Pilot plan (concept, not an approved government arrangement): the "Hub + Pavilion" prototype segment is the first pilot area; responsibility and collaborators, preconditions, data dictionary, qualitative resource bands, KPI decision gates, exit/rollback and annual recomputation flows are tabled in proposal.md; actors cover government, enterprises, universities, residents and communities, operators, volunteers, merchants and professional teams; a "lead + collaborate" conceptual RACI and the four-program annual system (Indicator Open Day, Progress Briefing, Monitoring Post Experience Week, International Indicator Methods Exchange) are detailed in proposal.md; every program must pass human gates for resources, rights, privacy and accessibility.
