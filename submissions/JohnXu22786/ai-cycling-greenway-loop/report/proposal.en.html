Land Use, Building Scale, and Retain-Renovate-Demolish Strategy

Retain-renovate-demolish is balanced with retention first: heritage-park fabric and historic relics are preserved, stock buildings undergo functional conversion with reserved directions for cycling-service retrofit, and demolition is limited to case-by-case minor clearance for slow-traffic connectivity. Stations and experience facilities are light, reversible and low-footprint; no large-scale buildings are added. All areas are concept caliber with a single recompute rule (the ring green band is aggregated into parkland) and are recalculated after official data; no FAR or building-height conclusions are given.

Blue-Green Network, Public Space, and Urban Character

The blue-green public-space system follows "ring band as the axis, nodes as pearls": the ring green band links pocket green spaces and open areas; stations and experience facilities blend with the park, light, transparent and reversible, without blocking rail-heritage relics or green-band sightlines. The public-space component kit (concept) includes rest seating, bike racks, drinking points, sign posts, shade canopies, child-friendly cycling grounds and all-age accessible ramps. The character continues the industrial-memory vocabulary of the Jing-Zhang Railway in orderly dialogue with new AI facilities; overall guidance is restrained and unified.

Renewal Projects, Implementation Policy, and Phasing

Three conceptual project lists (loop segments / station-chain mechanisms / service ecosystem) use advisory scale and investment expressions. Phasing: near term (1-3 years) pilots two segments (Origin Loop Gallery and Zhizao Cycle Port) with model stations; mid term (3-5 years) connects the full ring and establishes the Ride-Sharing Charter, rotating volunteer stations and Green-Ring Points mechanisms; far term (5-10 years) matures the service ecosystem and brand assets. Actors include government, enterprises, universities, residents, communities, volunteers, merchants and professional teams, with defined lead and collaborator roles (concept RACI). Pilots have stop and exit conditions (station removal and decommissioning triggers). Policy suggestions require legal review and are not government or operator commitments; a resident feedback channel and annual publicity are established.
