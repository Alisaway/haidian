# JINGZHANG AI CYCLING GREENWAY LOOP - Centennial Wheel Traces: A Slow-Traffic Loop Linking the Three Areas and Two Wings (RIDE·JZ LOOP)

**Primary name (EN lock-up): RIDE·JZ LOOP / Jingzhang AI Cycling Greenway Loop**
**Chinese counterpart: «京张AI骑行绿道环» (the Chinese master name, quoted gloss only).** This is an original concept: a cycling-and-walking greenway loop along the centennial Jing-Zhang railway corridor that links the three mandated areas (the Zhongzhiyuan AI Acceleration Area, the AI Origin Community, and the Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster) and stitches the two wings (the Zhongguancun Technology-Service Wing and the Xiaoyuehe Scenario-Empowerment Wing) through two side arcs, hosting shared stations and AI scenario nodes along the ring. All content is a concept proposal / reference plan for professional teams to deepen; it is based on a provisional boundary and must be recomputed when official data is published.

The source list includes: the organizer qualification announcement (2026-05-09) and agent open-call taskbook (2026-05-18), the Urban Design Administrative Measures, the statutory scope note on regulatory detailed planning, the public subset of the Land-Use Classification Guide, the provisional boundary geometry, the Interim Measures for Generative AI Services, the Accessible Environment Law, public railway-heritage materials (Jing-Zhang Railway 1909 opening, Jing-Zhang Railway Heritage Park, Zhongguancun development history), and public greenway/cycling cases (Copenhagen Supercykelstier, Amsterdam cycling network, Paris RER V, London Cycleways, Taiwan Cycling Route No.1, Chengdu Tianfu Greenway, Hangzhou greenway network). This is the working basis list; the actual data used is declared in sources.json, and gaps are recorded honestly in the assumptions and missing-data notes.

## Three-Level Scope Framework

The three levels transmit progressively: the coordinated research scope reads the corridor's industry, transport and blue-green resources and produces the overall judgement of a loop linking the three areas and two wings; the overall design scope organizes the cycling/walking slow-traffic system, station chain and public space along the loop; the key-area detailed design focuses on the three loop nodes (Zhizao Cycle Port, Origin Loop Gallery, Loop Market). Every level keeps machine-readable evidence anchors for the corresponding brief section; all scopes are conceptual and set no statutory-plan conclusions.

> **Evidence anchor:** [source:DATA-SRC-AGENT-TASKBOOK-2026-05-18].

## Coordinated Research Area: Industry and Future City Research

The coordinated research identifies the stitching value of a cycling/walking slow-traffic link across the three areas and two wings: rail heritage, blue-green space and AI-industry elements along the Jing-Zhang corridor are organized into a perceivable, experienceable public link that gives daily expression to the five functions (full-stack independent AI innovation, world-class AI innovation ecology, AI+ scenario empowerment, intelligent vital AI city, and global AI-governance discourse). Future-city research is directional only and implies no land, capacity or investment commitments.

> **Evidence anchor:** [source:DATA-SRC-PROVISIONAL-BOUNDARIES-2026-06-05].

## Overall Design Area: Urban Renewal and Regulatory-Plan-Level Urban Design

The overall design follows the principle "ring as the vein, stations as pearls": the loop's main line crosses the three area nodes, and the two side arcs stitch the Zhongguancun service wing and the Xiaoyuehe scenario wing; along the ring sit the Shuzhi Station-Chain (maintenance, water, charging, lockers; reversible modular facilities), the HuanYu AI wayfinding system, and a cycling-friendly cross-section concept. Renewal rather than new construction is emphasized: light, reversible cycling-service facilities are inserted into existing stock. Regulatory indicators are only checked as suggestions; no numeric conclusions are preset.

> **Evidence anchor:** [source:DATA-SRC-MOHURD-CONTROL-DETAILED-PLANNING], [source:DATA-SRC-PROVISIONAL-BOUNDARIES-2026-06-05].

## Detailed Design of Key Areas

All three loop nodes are conceptual locations that must be re-sited after site survey, data verification, competent-authority assessment and public comment: Zhizao Cycle Port (Zhongzhiyuan - full-stack AI independence: ride-test segment and maker experience), Origin Loop Gallery (AI Origin Community - railway-heritage narrative and AI culture gallery), and Loop Market (Dazhongsi - native-AI consumption and cycling lifestyle). They form a complete service loop with plans and sections expressed in the drawings and geometry layers.

## AI Innovation Ecosystem, Personas, and AI+ Scenarios

Twelve AI scenario cards sit along the loop (scenario-space-operation mapping): AI wayfinding, dock-scheduling, safety alerts, ride achievements, station services, rail interchange, cultural events, consumption experience, science explanation, data dashboard, emergency linkage and volunteer coordination; five persona groups are configured (daily commuter, campus student, family and elderly, visitor tourist, industry worker). All scenarios use anonymous aggregation only, human review for key decisions, and no individual-identification tracking or excessive surveillance. The three-sentence AI governance boundary is stated: anonymous aggregation only; key decisions human-reviewed; no over-surveillance. The mechanisms are concept suggestions and do not replace statutory duties.

> **Evidence anchor:** [source:DATA-SRC-GENERATIVE-AI-INTERIM-MEASURES].

## Land Use, Building Scale, and Retain-Renovate-Demolish Strategy

Retain-renovate-demolish is balanced with retention first: heritage-park fabric and historic relics are preserved, stock buildings undergo functional conversion with reserved directions for cycling-service retrofit, and demolition is limited to case-by-case minor clearance for slow-traffic connectivity. Stations and experience facilities are light, reversible and low-footprint; no large-scale buildings are added. All areas are concept caliber with a single recompute rule (the ring green band is aggregated into parkland) and are recalculated after official data; no FAR or building-height conclusions are given.

> **Evidence anchor:** [source:DATA-SRC-MNR-LAND-USE-CLASSIFICATION-202311].

## Transport, Rail, Municipal Infrastructure, and Public Services

Slow-traffic priority organizes the transport concept: the loop crosses the three nodes and connects to rail and bus stops; event-day crowd organization is integrated with the slow-traffic system. Cycling-friendly supports are all concept-level: HuanYu signage wayfinding, Wheel-Trace Ride Card unified dock-and-return, station services, and AI safety alerts. Municipal works assume reuse of existing conditions with no power-load, network-capacity or engineering-quantity conclusions. Public services include multilingual information, cycling-science and workshop space; all AI decisions have human review.

> **Evidence anchor:** [source:DATA-SRC-MOHURD-URBAN-DESIGN-MEASURES].

## Blue-Green Network, Public Space, and Urban Character

The blue-green public-space system follows "ring band as the axis, nodes as pearls": the ring green band links pocket green spaces and open areas; stations and experience facilities blend with the park, light, transparent and reversible, without blocking rail-heritage relics or green-band sightlines. The public-space component kit (concept) includes rest seating, bike racks, drinking points, sign posts, shade canopies, child-friendly cycling grounds and all-age accessible ramps. The character continues the industrial-memory vocabulary of the Jing-Zhang Railway in orderly dialogue with new AI facilities; overall guidance is restrained and unified.

> **Evidence anchor:** [source:DATA-SRC-MOHURD-URBAN-DESIGN-MEASURES].

## Renewal Projects, Implementation Policy, and Phasing

Three conceptual project lists (loop segments / station-chain mechanisms / service ecosystem) use advisory scale and investment expressions. Phasing: near term (1-3 years) pilots two segments (Origin Loop Gallery and Zhizao Cycle Port) with model stations; mid term (3-5 years) connects the full ring and establishes the Ride-Sharing Charter, rotating volunteer stations and Green-Ring Points mechanisms; far term (5-10 years) matures the service ecosystem and brand assets. Actors include government, enterprises, universities, residents, communities, volunteers, merchants and professional teams, with defined lead and collaborator roles (concept RACI). Pilots have stop and exit conditions (station removal and decommissioning triggers). Policy suggestions require legal review and are not government or operator commitments; a resident feedback channel and annual publicity are established.
