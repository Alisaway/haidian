Slow mobility

Three conceptual routes: quiet listening route + commuting route + theater route, combined with existing slow-mobility systems, green belts and rail-station connection directions (conceptual structure, not road red lines). Rail-source and quiet-zone separation uses buffer green belts, terrain and noise-barrier directions (concept; no noise values or quantities).

Blue-green public space

The Jing-Zhang Railway Heritage Park green belt and the Xiaoyue River direction are the conceptual tie; quiet zones are placed in green depth. Public-space component kit: quiet pavilions, sound benches, ear trumpets, soundscape signs, movable sound-field devices, low-noise lighting - durable, repairable, relocatable, traceable and offline-capable; the Quiet Convention operates by disclosure and voluntary commitment.

Buildings

Stock-first, reversible light upgrades: the Memory Hall favors stock retrofitting; the Theater favors lightweight demountable structure. No FAR, height or gross-scale figures are given. Demolition/retention follows a four-step determination; no demolition area is proposed before current-condition and ownership surveys.

Renewal projects

Three project lists: archive & display / quiet-listening network / acoustic governance; phases are near term 1-3 years, mid term 3-5 years, long term 5-10 years (concept rhythm); annual program brands: Soundwalk Day, Oral-history Week, Sound Art Festival (pilot). Pilot governance annex (RACI, data dictionary, aggregation threshold, retention, access/deletion, human review, complaint/correction, service level, stage acceptance and exit conditions) is in section 10 of the proposal.

AI scenarios

Twelve AI+ scenario cards: noise-monitoring, soundscape guide, soundscape archive, event sound-control, quiet-keeping, semantic annotation, oral-history transcription, sound-rights assist, quiet-zone patrol work orders, post-event re-silencing, soundscape health science and co-creation AI; organized across six domains (transport, industry, space, public services, culture, governance); anonymized aggregation only, human review of key decisions, no excessive monitoring (no individual-identification tracking); three industry test protocols with model-evaluation and runtime-monitoring processes.
