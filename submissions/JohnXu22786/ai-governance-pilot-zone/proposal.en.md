# ZHI-REG GREEN LOOP - AI GOVERNANCE PILOT ZONE (Concept)

**English convenience summary** (full binding narrative is the Chinese proposal.md; this summary may lag the zh text).

以「智治绿廊（Zhi-Reg Green Loop）」组织AI治理试验段概念：沿京张铁路遗址公园绿带布设一个可感知、可参与、可复算的AI治理试验平台，把「治理」从文件与后台转译为绿带中的公共体验，构成百年京张AI创新带的治理话语权试验段；「一带三段、三节点」——治理实验室（AI治理公共实验室：试验+展示+公众参与）、算法审计塔（算法审计与影响评估枢纽）、标识步道（合成内容标识示范步道：标识艺术展+治理开放数据集），六类AI+场景（治理场景沙盒、算法影响评估先行区、合成内容标识示范步道、公众参与决策沙盒、城市AI观测试验段、AI治理开放数据集）仅处理匿名聚合数据、关键决策人工复核、禁止过度监控，交通慢行优先贯通绿带，与遗址公园文旅场景及相邻园区AI创新场景低干预衔接，分期实施+活动体系（AI治理开放日、算法审计公开周、合成内容标识艺术展）。全部为概念建议、参考方案，基于 provisional 边界，官方数据发布后复算。

## Outline
- Zhi-Reg Green Loop - AI Governance Pilot Zone: an AI-governance experimentation platform along the Jingzhang Railway Heritage Park green belt, turning governance into public experience.
- Node 1 - Governance Lab: AI-governance public laboratory in the green belt (experiment + exhibition + public participation).
- Node 2 - Algorithm Audit Tower: algorithm audit and impact-assessment hub.
- Node 3 - Marker Trail: synthetic-content marker demonstration trail (marker art expo + open governance dataset).
- 6 AI+ scenarios (governance scenario sandbox, algorithmic impact-assessment pilot, synthetic-content marker trail, public-participation decision sandbox, urban AI observation section, open governance dataset); anonymized aggregation only, key decisions human-reviewed, no over-surveillance.
- Slow-traffic-first green-belt route linking heritage park cultural scenes and adjacent park AI innovation scenes via low-intervention facilities.
- Phased implementation and event system (AI governance open day, algorithm audit public week, synthetic-content marker art expo).
- All figures are concept suggestions on a provisional boundary; recompute when official data is published.
