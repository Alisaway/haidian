Three-tier scopes

Coordinated research scope: functional depth of the pilot zone (official wording 43.6 km2); overall design scope: 11.4 km2 (official wording, green-belt pilot sequence); key areas: Zhongzhiyuan ~192 ha, Beijing AI Origin Community ~104 ha, Dazhongsi ~72 ha (three zones + two wings, official wording). This package is a linear concept sub-topic spanning the overall-design and key-area tiers; all boundaries are provisional indications to be fully recomputed after official geometry release.

Key areas

Three concept nodes: (1) Turnout Lab (governance lab: experiment + exhibit + participation), (2) Signal Tower (algorithm audit tower: audit + impact assessment), (3) Waymark Walk (marker trail: synthetic-content labeling + open dataset). Each node defines its space type, siting criteria and relocation triggers: whenever official boundaries, ownership, setbacks, heritage or rail-protection-zone limits conflict with the concept seat, the node repositions along the green belt. The reversible public-space component library and the honor-display system respond to agent.4.

Mobility

Slow-traffic first: continuous walking + cycling through the belt (concept main route ~7.8 km), no motorized through traffic; three east-west stitch/entry paths with all-age barrier-free design; nodes sited within walkable reach of Line 13 station contexts (stations per official materials); shared sensing poles with anonymized aggregates only; no individual-identifying observation on the belt. Municipal works retrofit existing networks only, without new large stations.

Blue-green public space

The Jingzhang Railway Heritage Park green belt is the blue-green skeleton and public-space spine; the "governance lab - audit tower - marker trail" sequence stays continuous and open; sponge rainwater gardens and tree-lined places sit at the stitch points. Character direction "rail memory + digital order"; the cultural wayfinding system is bilingual and readability-first, with international-communication copy ("governance makes AI trustworthy and visible"). Barrier-free routes, age-friendly seating and child-friendly signage follow all-age standards; human desks cover elders and low-digital-skill residents.

Buildings

Retention-renovation-demolition in parallel: railway and industrial heritage concept elements retained as far as possible; old corridor buildings undergo functional replacement and light renovation (volumes capped by existing footprints); demolition limited to verified dangerous and illegal buildings, handled case by case. The first row along the belt stays low and transparent, with massing increasing toward block interiors; all areas are concept calibers to be recomputed after official release. The component library uses standardized reversible modules, rotated with events and fully restored to green on removal.

Renewal projects

Six renewal projects (concept): green-belt governance-infrastructure retrofit, Turnout Lab retrofit and operation, Signal Tower retrofit, Waymark Walk trail upgrade, Platform Assembly Hall siting and construction, and corridor building functional replacement with shared facilities. Phasing: near term 1-3 years (licensing pilot), mid term 3-5 years (networked governance), far term 5-10 years (rule output). Lead/collaborator roles, cost tiers (qualitative low/medium/high), pilot duration, human backstop, acceptance indicators, appeal channels, exit conditions and long-term KPIs are in the implementation matrices of proposal.en.md; the stop condition is "suspend - remove - restore green". No government or investment commitment is implied.

AI scenarios

12 scenario cards cover six AI+ scenario families (governance sandbox, algorithmic-impact-assessment pilot, synthetic-content marker trail, public-participation decision sandbox, urban AI observation section, open governance dataset); each card states users / input data / AI capability / spatial carrier / operator / human backstop / evaluation metrics. Launch requires the three test protocols (model evaluation, data quality, runtime monitoring) with human review points throughout. All scenarios follow "minimum collection, purpose limitation, anonymized aggregation, human review"; anonymized aggregates only, key decisions human-reviewed, no over-surveillance. Developer community, scenario-opening and conversion mechanisms, honor-display system, barrier-free guide pavilion and digital-twin sandbox are part of the scenario system.
