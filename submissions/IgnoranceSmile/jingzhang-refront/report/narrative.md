# 正式包阅读说明 / Formal Package Reading Note

本包以“一园双面，三区两翼”为总体母图，以 AI 原点复面样段为旗舰，并通过众智园科研前场、大钟寺抵达前沿、四个英雄场景、三个公共记录地标和十个项目包把空间、运营与 AI 治理组织在同一套图纸里。`proposal.md` 是完整中文正文，`proposal.en.md` 为逐节对应英文版；A0/A3 与双语 HTML 是主要评审入口，GeoJSON、metrics、sources 和三类矩阵承担复算与证据核验。

所有设计均为证据约束下的概念建议。`concept_design_eligible=true`，但 `engineering_implementation_verified=false`；门、柱网、权属、消防、停车、后勤、法定红线和实施批准未被伪装成既成事实。任何工程推进都必须先完成专业核验，并守住公园净公共空间不减、普通通行能力不降、既有生态绩效不退和必要后勤端点不断线。

The package uses “One Park, Two Fronts, Three Zones and Two Service Wings” as its spatial mother diagram. The complete Chinese and English narratives correspond section by section; A0/A3 and the bilingual offline HTML are the primary review entry points, while GeoJSON, metrics, sources and the three matrices provide recalculation and evidence layers. All spatial moves are evidence-constrained concept proposals. Engineering implementation remains unverified and locked pending professional review.
