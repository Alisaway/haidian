Method before sites: how AI Agents enter planning

Jingzhang does not use AI to repackage a conventional plan. Its heritage, innovation networks, everyday neighbourhoods and long-term public realm are used to test whether Agents can produce traceable and correctable planning judgements.

CORE ARGUMENT
One argument chain: three questions (how planning is produced | how urban content changes | how outputs keep operating) → two loops (evidence–computation ⇄ value–deliberation) → seven planning questions (sense–define–simulate–spatialise–deliberate–implement–communicate) → six official tasks → Jingzhang stress test → six validity conditions.
