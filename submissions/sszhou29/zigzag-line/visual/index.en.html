Key Areas

368.4 ha (three) · Three sub-areas

How particular places are made -- land use, function, buildings, delivery.

The three belts are not three parallel strips: the Centennial Jing-Zhang Cultural Belt (c. 100-200 m either side of the heritage line), the Urban AI Living Experience Belt (c. 500 m), and the AI Convergence Innovation Belt (design scope out to the study area) -- all three radiate outward from the heritage line as gradients, not in parallel.

Key Areas

The brief assigns each of the three zones and two wings an official role; this proposal's production/living/services division classifies modes of spatial contact. The two dimensions are mapped explicitly, not a rewrite of the official positioning.

key_areas.geojson

Zhongzhiyuan AI Acceleration Zone (192.1 ha)

Official role: Full-stack self-reliant AI innovation system and a global voice in AI governance | This proposal: Production / testing

Primary benchmark: King's Cross + Pittsburgh Hazelwood Green

A voice in AI governance is not produced by declaration but by citable practice -- a regime of "testing that can be vetoed," with veto records published.

Beijing AI Origin Community (104.3 ha)

Official role: World-class AI innovation ecosystem | This proposal: Living / co-habitation

Primary benchmark: Kendall Square

A protection priority, not a renewal priority -- 6% vacancy, close to fully let; the market has endorsed it, and forcing renewal would damage rather than improve it.

Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster (72.0 ha)

Official role: Intelligent-native new business formats | This proposal: Services / exchange

Primary benchmark: Station F

The heaviest existing commercial stock and densest incumbent micro-enterprise -- the front line of the anti-displacement mechanism, where the >=70% five-year retention commitment lands.
