From four renewal actions to three public promises

The previous drawing explains how space is renewed. This board explains why the three key areas exist and what each promises to the public. They are not three isolated objects, but a continuous mechanism from idea initiation and evidence validation to value return.

Spatial sequence: AI Origin Community initiates ideas, Zhongzhiyuan carries validation and responsibility, and Dazhongsi supports conversion and public return. The three node designs follow below, beginning with Zhongzhiyuan · Validation Lighthouse.

Zhongzhiyuan · Validation Lighthouse

A garden-style innovation district combining national platforms, industry display, Qinghe culture and low-carbon social space into a visible, reviewable validation gateway.

Beijing AI Origin Community · Co-intelligence Origin

A university-adjacent conversion district whose east and west gateways connect open-source release, shared living rooms and talent services across daily and event modes.

Dazhongsi · Return Commons

An urban intelligent-economy district where the Return Commons connects leading firms, agent-native businesses, commercial services and four-quadrant slow mobility.
